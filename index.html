FAQ & Legal Questions

Quick answers about consultations, requests, communication and escrow.

Frequently asked questions

How can I book a lawyer?

Open Find Lawyer, choose a lawyer and submit a consultation request with your preferred date and basic case details.

How does escrow work?

Payment is held securely while the legal service is in progress and becomes available for release after the service reaches the required completion stage.

Can I follow my request status?

Yes. The Client Dashboard shows active requests, appointment information and recent activity.

Where do I communicate with my lawyer?

Use the Chat page to keep case-related messages together with the relevant lawyer.

Can I share documents?

The dashboard includes a document area for files connected to your legal request.

What should I include in a complaint?

Add a clear subject, the relevant service or request, and enough detail for the support team to understand the issue.

Need more help?

If your question is related to your account or a platform issue, contact the Law Pulse support team.
